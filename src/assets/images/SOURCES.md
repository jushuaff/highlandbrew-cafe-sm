# Highland Brew Concept Asset Notes

This concept site is prepared as a proposal and demo experience for Highland Brew Cafe - SM City Baguio. The project avoids fabricated commercial claims and keeps content grounded in public business references.

## Current asset approach
- The approved Highland Brew logo is loaded from `src/assets/logos/highland-brew.png`.
- The homepage uses locally downloaded Highland Brew images from the public references below; these should be reviewed for final usage rights before production deployment.

## Recommended replacement process
1. Keep the approved logo at `src/assets/logos/highland-brew.png`
2. Confirm image permissions before deployment
3. Update alt text and captions with final approved photography details
4. Remove the demo note only after the business approves the concept
